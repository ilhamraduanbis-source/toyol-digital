1. FOG
I borrow the streets but own no stone.
I wrap each streetlamp in a silver gown.
When sunshine climbs, I leave no footprints.
Answer: Fog

2. HAIL
Clouds make small marbles without a mold.
They rattle your roof, but never settle in.
Hold one too long, and you are holding rain.
Answer: Hail

3. WIND
No feet, yet I race through the reeds.
No hands, yet I open the gate.
I give a kite its wings, but own no sky.
Answer: Wind

4. FROST
I grow white gardens without a seed.
My blossoms cling to glass at dawn.
A warm fingertip makes them disappear.
Answer: Frost

5. THUNDER
I follow a bright flash without a torch.
I make the windows tremble, but touch no pane.
You hear my arrival before I am gone.
Answer: Thunder
